Approach

How We Work

Significant technology decisions rarely exist in isolation. They involve business priorities, technology realities, organisational capabilities, and choices whose consequences extend well beyond the immediate decision.

Sapience brings business understanding, technology expertise, and long-term organisational perspective to these decisions.

The Sapience Decision Framework

This framework provides a structured approach to understanding, evaluating, and defining significant technology decisions.

It adapts to the nature and significance of the decision; not every engagement requires every stage to the same depth.

01

Understand

The context

What is the organisation trying to achieve, and why does the decision matter?

We begin with the business — its strategy, priorities, ambitions, customers, constraints, and organisational context.

02

Assess

The reality

Where is the organisation today?

We establish an objective view of the current technology landscape, capabilities, risks, complexity, and constraints.

03

Envision

The possibilities

What could the organisation become?

We explore future possibilities and the technology capabilities that could enable the organisation's ambitions, without prematurely committing to a particular solution.

04

Evaluate

The choices

What alternatives exist, and what are their implications?

We evaluate choices through business value, technology fit, investment, risk, organisational capability, and long-term strategic fit.

05

Define the Direction

The path forward

What does the decision mean for the organisation going forward?

We help translate the decision into a coherent direction — establishing priorities, sequencing, roadmap considerations, principles, and the capabilities required to move forward.

How the Framework is Applied

The framework is applied through three perspectives: Business, Technology, and Organisation.

Business

Does the decision strengthen the organisation's strategy, customer proposition, business capabilities, and long-term objectives?

Technology

Is the technology direction sound, sustainable, resilient, and capable of supporting the organisation's ambitions?

Organisation

Can the organisation develop, operate, govern, and sustain what the technology decision requires?

Business defines the ambition. Technology enables it. Organisation sustains it.

Leadership Outcomes

The framework helps leadership move from complexity to clarity, conviction, and confidence, giving them the perspective to make the decision that is right for their organisation.

Clarity

A clear understanding of the context, current reality, available possibilities, and choices.

Conviction

A well-informed basis for choosing a direction and understanding its implications.

Confidence

A coherent direction and path forward.

Technology decisions are ultimately decisions about the organisation.
