Services

How We Help

Sapience provides independent technology advisory to organisations across the lending ecosystem — from established banks and NBFCs to emerging lending fintechs.

We help organisations make significant technology decisions — from defining a Technology Vision and modernising enterprise platforms to evaluating strategic technology investments and applying emerging technologies such as AI.

Our services combine strategic thinking, enterprise architecture expertise, and practical implementation experience to help organisations make significant technology decisions with clarity, confidence, and a long-term perspective.

Our Advisory Services

Every organisation's priorities are different. Our work is shaped by the decisions an organisation needs to make, rather than a predefined consulting methodology.

Depending on the organisation's context, we help across the following areas.

01

Technology Vision

Define a clear Technology Vision that guides significant technology decisions and ensures technology continues to support the organisation's strategic direction.

02

Technology Strategy

Translate business priorities into a technology strategy that aligns investments, capabilities, and execution with long-term business objectives.

03

Enterprise Architecture

Establish an architectural foundation that enables technology to evolve in a structured, resilient, and sustainable manner.

04

Technology Leadership Advisory

Strengthen technology leadership by helping organisations define technology strategy, build internal capability, and establish the governance and decision-making needed to support sustainable business growth.

05

Platform Evaluation & Selection

Evaluate enterprise platforms and technology partners with an independent perspective, balancing immediate business needs with long-term strategic fit.

06

Technology Modernisation

Modernise technology landscapes in a way that reduces complexity, manages risk, and enables future growth.

07

AI Strategy

Identify where AI can create meaningful business value and develop a practical, business-led approach to its adoption across the organisation.

08

Technology Governance

Strengthen technology decision-making through governance that improves alignment, transparency, and long-term outcomes.

09

Technology Due Diligence

Evaluate technology capabilities, architecture, delivery maturity, and technology-related risks to support significant business decisions.

Significant technology decisions rarely exist in isolation.

Defining a Technology Vision influences technology strategy. Strategy shapes enterprise architecture. Architecture affects platform decisions. Technology governance determines how those decisions are sustained. AI strategy depends upon the capabilities the organisation has already built.

Our role is not simply to advise on individual initiatives. It is to help organisations make significant technology decisions within the context of a coherent long-term direction, ensuring that every decision strengthens the organisation they are building.
